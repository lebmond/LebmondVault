This CFA Level III lesson covers Capital Market Expectations (CME): Framework and Macro Considerations, with a focus on how macroeconomic analysis supports strategic asset allocation and portfolio management. You’ll learn how economic growth, business cycles, inflation, monetary and fiscal policy, interest rates, the yield curve, and exchange rates influence capital market expectations and asset returns.  
  
The lesson also explains challenges in economic forecasting, approaches to forecasting economic conditions, the impact of exogenous shocks, the relationship between business cycles and asset prices, and how monetary and fiscal policy can affect economic growth. It also covers the Taylor rule, zero and negative interest rates, yield curve dynamics, and interest rate and exchange rate relationships.  
  
What you’ll learn:  
✓ How Capital Market Expectations support strategic asset allocation

✓ Challenges and limitations in economic forecasting  
✓ How exogenous shocks can affect economic growth

✓ Major approaches to economic forecasting  
✓ How business cycles affect short- and long-term expectations  
✓ How inflation affects cash, bonds, equities, and real estate  
✓ How monetary and fiscal policy influence the economy  
✓ How the Taylor rule guides short-term interest rate decisions  
✓ The implications of zero and negative interest rates  
✓ How the business cycle influences the shape of the yield curve  
✓ The relationship between interest rates, capital flows, and exchange rates
