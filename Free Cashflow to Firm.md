[[Free Cashflow to Firm|FCFF]] is the cash flow available to all of the firm's investors including debtholders and sharholders.

When doing valuation using the [[Free Cashflow to Firm|FCFF]], the required rate of return is WACC because we need to consider both shareholders' and debt holders' required rate of return weighted by their investment.
# Formula
There are multiple formulas can be used to calcualte the [[Free Cashflow to Firm|FCFF]]
$$

\text{FCFF}=\text{NI}+\text{NCC}+int(1-T)-\text{WC}_{\text{Inv}}-\text{FC}_{\text{Inv}}
$$
$$
\text{FCFF}=\text{EBIT}(1-T)+\text{NCC}-\text{WC}_{\text{Inv}}-\text{FC}_{\text{Inv}}
$$
$$
\text{FCFF}=\text{EBITDA}(1-T)+\text{NCC}\cdot T-\text{WC}_{\text{Inv}}-\text{FC}_{\text{Inv}}
$$
$$
\text{FCFF}=\text{CFO}+int(1-T)-\text{FC}_{\text{Inv}}
$$
注意的小公式:
$CFO = NI + NCC - WC_{inv}$
[[Cashflow from Operation]] = [[Net Income]] + [[Non-Cash Charge]] - [[Working Capital Investment]]
