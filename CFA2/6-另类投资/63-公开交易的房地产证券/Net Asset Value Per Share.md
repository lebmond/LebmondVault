> [!NOTE] 
> Generally, NAVPS is considered the **most appropriate measure of REITs' & REOCs' values.**

# 计算公式
$$\text{NAVPS}=\frac{\text{Market Value of Assets} - \text{Market value of Liabilities} }{\text{Number of Shares}}$$
where Market Value of Assets can be further calculated by:
$$\frac{\text{Estimated Cash Net Operating Income next year}}{\text{Capitalization Rate}}+ \text{Cash}+\text{AR}+\text{Other Assets}$$
注意的是这里的[[Net Operating Income|net operating income]]是仅**cash**的,即需要扣除**non-cash rent**, 再加回**full-year adjustment for acquisition**
