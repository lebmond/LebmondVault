商品的期货价格 < 现货价格
由short hedgers (producers of the commodity who short futures to **protect against price decreases**)主导的市场通常是backwardation 这也被称为normal backwardation

# Connection with [[Calendar Spread]]
A commodity market in backwardation has a positive [[Calendar Spread|calendar spread]], and the opposite for [[Contango]].
