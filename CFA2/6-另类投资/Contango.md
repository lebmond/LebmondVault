商品的期货价格 > 现货价格
对于可以长期存放的商品(或者市场由long hedgers, **users of the commodity who buy futures to protect against price increases**主导),通常市场是contango

# Connection with [[Calendar Spread]]
A commodity market in Contango has a negative[[Calendar Spread|calendar spread]], and the opposite for [[Backwardation]].
