## Formula
**特别值得注意的是,虽然名字叫做TED Spread,但是T-bill yield是被减数,放在了MRR之后**
$$\text{TED Spread}=\text{3 Month MRR} - \text{3 month T-Bill Yield}$$

An increase in the TED spread indicates that the investors believe **the risk of default** on interbank loans. 
Compared to 10-year [[Swap Spread]]. the TED Spread more accurately reflects the **risk in the banking system**.
