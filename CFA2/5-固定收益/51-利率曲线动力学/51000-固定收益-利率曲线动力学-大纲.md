# YTM
YTM for a coupon bond is the rate of return of a coupon bond under three assumptions:
1. The bond is held to maturity;
2. No default or delay happen to the coupon or principal payment;
3. The coupon payment is re-invested at the same rate of return as this bond.

## Relationship with Market Yields
When market yields drop, coupons reinvest at lower rates, causing realized yield < YTM even if the bond is held to maturity. This is an example of [[reinvestment risk]]. But if the investor does not hold the bond to maturity, then the realized return depends on both:
- [[price risk]]: **Sale price** at the time of liquidation, as the bond price will fluctuate in the opposite direction of market yields movement.
- [[reinvestment risk]]: **Reinvestment rate of coupons received up to sale**

当以下情况发生时，YTM 可能会成为对预期收益率的糟糕估计：
1. 利率高度波动；
2. 收益率曲线陡峭，无论是向上倾斜还是向下倾斜。


# [[TED Spread]]


When the spot curve is upward sloping and its level and shape are expected to remain constant over an investment horizon, buying bonds with a maturity longer than the investment horizon (i.e. [[Riding the Yield Curve]]）will provide a total return greater than the return on a maturity-matching strategy.
