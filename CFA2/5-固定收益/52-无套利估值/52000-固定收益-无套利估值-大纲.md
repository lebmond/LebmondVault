# Interest Rate Tree
Often a lognormal tree is used to model **forward rate**. There are two advantages: (1) non-negative interest rate; (2) higher volatility at higher rate.
### Two Assumptions
1. $i_{H}=i_{L}\exp(2\sigma)$, where $\sigma$ is the periodic standard deviation of interest rate.
2. Equal probabilities to get one of the two possible forward rates
