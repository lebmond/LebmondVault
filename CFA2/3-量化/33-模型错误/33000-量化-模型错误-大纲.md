# Model Mispecification
## [[Omitted Variable]] 遗漏变量
会导致出现[[Heteroskedasticity]]或者[[Serial Correlation]]
## Inappropriate Form of Varaibles
会导致出现[[Heteroskedasticity]]

## Inappropriate Variable Scaling
会导致出现[[Heteroskedasticity]]或者[[Multi-Collinearity]]
## [[Incorrect Data Pooling]]
Regression model pools data from different samples that should not be pooled
会导致出现[[Heteroskedasticity]]或者[[Serial Correlation]]
