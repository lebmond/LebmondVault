# Assumption
1. [[Relative Purchase Power Parity]] holds in both the short and long runs. 


an _X_ percent rise in the domestic money supply will produce an _X_ percent rise in the domestic price level. Assuming that [[Relative Purchase Power Parity]] holds, a money supply–induced increase (decrease) in domestic prices relative to foreign prices should lead to a proportional decrease (increase) in the domestic currency’s value. 
换句话说,在[[Pure Monetary Approach]]下,宽松的货币政策会使得本国的通胀水平上升,进而使得货币贬值.

# 缺陷
Because purchasing power parity rarely holds in either the short or medium run, the pure monetary model may not provide a realistic explanation of the impact of monetary forces on the exchange rate.
这个短期和中期内的缺点一定程度上能够被[[Dornbusch Exchange Rate Overshooting Model]]所揭示.
