# Underlying Assumptions
1. Options are European style;
2. The underlying return follows a geometric Brownian Motion with constant drift and volatility;
3. The market is frictionless with no transaction costs, regulatory constraints or taxes and no arbitrage opportunity;
4. The risk-free rate is constant;
5. No dividends are paid out during the life of the option (though modified versions like the [Macroption Black-Scholes Guide](https://www.macroption.com/black-scholes-formula/) account for dividend yields).

$d_{1}$: $N(d_{1})$ is the delta of the call option 
$d_2$: the risk-neutral probability that the option will end in the money.
